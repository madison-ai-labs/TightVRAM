Bench system 1

A gaming PC turned headless inference server. Nothing about it is special, which is why it is worth measuring.

GPURTX 3070, 8 GB GDDR6, headless, so all 8 GB are usable
LinkPCIe 3.0 x16. The card and the Z590 board could do Gen4, the Comet Lake CPU can't
CPUIntel Core i9-10850K, 10 cores / 20 threads
RAM2 × 16 GB DDR4-3000 (XMP), single rank, dual channel
OSUbuntu 26.04, headless
Runtimellama.cpp built from source, CUDA 12.4, driver 595
ModelQwen3.6-35B-A3B, Unsloth UD-Q4_K_XL, 22.4 GB. Control: Qwen3.5-9B Q4_K_M, 5.7 GB
