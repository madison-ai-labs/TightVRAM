INF-012, hypothesis

Breaking the PCIe bottleneck

Prefill on bench system 1 waits on expert uploads over PCIe 3.0. The card and the board can do Gen4, the CPU can't. We calculated what Gen4 would give, and a calculation is not a measurement.

Today, PCIe 3.0, measured
2,384 t/s
With PCIe 4.0, calculated
4,000 to 4,500 t/s

Needed: Intel i7-11700K or i9-11900K, about €150 to €180 used.

Memory bandwidth scaling

Generation speed here is set by how fast the CPU reads the active experts from RAM. XMP alone gave 15 to 24 %. A DDR5 platform would show how far that line continues.

Needed: DDR5 platform with 64 GB, as a donation or a loan.

What you get, and what you don't

  • The experiment is registered with its hypothesis before the hardware arrives.
  • The result is published with raw data, including when it refutes the hypothesis.
  • Supporters are named on the experiment page if they want to be.
  • Funding does not determine research conclusions.

Hardware loans, research partnerships and independent reproductions are as welcome as money. If you rerun one of our experiments on your own machine, that raises its evidence level to "independently reproduced", which nothing we do ourselves can.
