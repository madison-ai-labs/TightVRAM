  1. INF-012

    Does PCIe 4.0 remove the prefill bottleneck?

    Prefill on this machine waits on expert uploads over PCIe 3.0. The card and the board can do Gen4, the CPU can't. We calculated what a CPU swap would give. Nothing has been measured yet.

  2. INF-011

    Prefetching experts: +45 % prefill in 187 lines

    Prefill spends 59 to 65 % of its time uploading experts, and llama.cpp does that strictly before computing each layer. Loading the next layer's experts on a second CUDA stream gave +37 to +50 %, with bit-identical output. It's now in production.

    pp16k 1,629 → 2,438 t/s
    Measured✓ Kept
    25 Nov 2026
  3. INF-010

    Quant formats as a transport problem

    We quantized real expert tensors of Qwen3.6 into 30 formats and measured error, CPU decode time, GPU time and upload cost for each. On this machine the right question isn't "how many bits" but "how many bytes per millisecond of unpacking".

    Q3_K: −21 % tg time, 2× the error
    Measured… Open
    25 Nov 2026
  4. INF-009

    The simulation said +23 %. The measurement said −4 %.

    We traced the expert routing of 43,000 tokens, simulated a fork that keeps hot experts on the GPU, and predicted +23 % generation speed. Measured, it was 4 % slower. Multi-token prediction gave a real +11 %, and we still left it out.

    predicted +23 %, measured −4.3 %
    Measured✕ Rejected
    25 Nov 2026
  5. INF-008

    The thinking loop that wasn't a loop

    A coding agent thought for 15 minutes and 32,894 tokens without calling a tool. 280 replays of that first step later, the endless loop turned out to be rare, and the model card's coding sampler halved the long tail.

    long runs 23 → 13 of 120
    Measured✓ Kept
    15 Nov 2026
  6. INF-007

    Why a screenshot takes 12 seconds

    Qwen3.6 reads screenshots fine with its vision projector on the CPU, but a 1280-pixel image costs 12 seconds before the first token. 81 % of that is the image encoder. On the GPU it takes 0.5 s, and it costs half the context.

    11.8 → 2.8 s
    Measured± Trade-off
    11 Nov 2026
  7. INF-006

    Is the uncensored model better with tools?

    A popular abliterated Qwen3.6-35B-A3B against the original on 28 tool-use scenarios. It scored lower, broke action limits, and in 3 of 3 runs followed a prompt injection that mailed a secrets file to the outside.

    77.5 vs 81.5 of 84
    Measured✕ Rejected
    9 Nov 2026
  8. INF-005

    gpu-unlock: +90 % on GEMM, +1 % on tokens

    A boot-time firmware exploit lifts the RTX 3070's halved tensor-core rate for FP16 with FP32 accumulation. Matrix multiplies got almost twice as fast. Our quantized LLM barely noticed, and image generation got 14 to 32 % faster.

    GEMM +90 %, pp +1–5 %
    Measured✕ Rejected
    6 Nov 2026
  9. INF-004

    Two knobs that made it slower, and why

    A VRAM cache for experts cut generation in half at a 0.07 % hit rate. N-gram speculation helped only when the answer repeated the prompt word for word, and it changed answers at temperature 0.

    tg −55 %
    Measured✕ Rejected
    6 Nov 2026
  10. INF-003

    Pinned memory: --load-mode none, auto and dio

    Switching the expert weights from a memory-mapped file to pinned memory made prefill 17 % faster and explained the mystery writes from the day before. It also brought swap bursts, a crash at 115k tokens, and a lesson about MemAvailable.

    pp +17 %, agent −11 %
    Measured✓ Kept
    6 Nov 2026
  11. INF-002

    XMP, and the RAM bandwidth that wasn't

    Turning on XMP gave 15 to 24 % more tokens per second. Then STREAM claimed the RAM got slower with more threads, and the explanation turned out to be the CPU's clock control, not the memory.

    tg 31.2 → 36.5 t/s
    Measured✓ Kept
    5 Nov 2026

    pp16k 560 → 1,511 t/s
