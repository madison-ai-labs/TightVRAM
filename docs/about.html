About the lab

Madison AI Labs is an independent research lab in Vienna. It has no investors and no product to sell. It has questions about how intelligence can run on less, remember more, and know what it knows.

How we work

A finding is not a truth because it was written down once
Every result carries an evidence level: hypothesis, measured, repeated, independently reproduced. The level only goes up with new data.
Measured, calculated, claimed
We ran it and have the raw data, or we derived it from measured parts, or it is someone else's number we haven't checked. Each statement says which.
Rules before results
The bar an idea has to clear is written down before the measurement. An idea that gives a real gain and still misses its bar is rejected, and the page says so.
Failures are results
Most ideas we tried did nothing or made things slower. They are in the registry next to the ones that worked, with the same detail.
Corrections stay visible
When something turns out wrong, the correction goes next to the original statement. Nothing is quietly edited away.
A person approves every release
Lab notes are written continuously, by people and by agents. What appears here was checked against its raw data and released by a human.
